The Process
How JanMitra Call Works
Designed for citizens who have never used the internet — just a basic phone call is enough.
📞
Step 01

Citizen Calls

A farmer or daily-wage worker dials our toll-free number from any phone — 2G feature phone works perfectly.

🎙️
Step 02

Speaks Naturally

The caller speaks their details in Hindi — age, occupation, income, state. No menus, no "press 1 for…"

🔊
Step 03

ASR Converts

Google Speech-to-Text transcribes the audio. The system understands accented Hindi and mixed Hinglish.

🧠
Step 04

Gemini Matches

Gemini API searches the verified scheme database using RAG — no hallucinations, only database-grounded answers.

🔈
Step 05

Speaks Back

The answer is converted to speech (TTS) and played back. "You qualify for PM-Kisan. Here's how to apply…"

📱
Step 06

SMS Sent

Twilio sends a follow-up SMS with the scheme name, official portal link, and required documents list.
